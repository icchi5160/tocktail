Time, kept on your PC.

Checking desktop connection…

Open the Tocktail desktop app to record your foreground site time. Other windows and private browsing stay outside site tracking when they cannot be verified.

Your browsing stays local

  • Site origins are sent without page paths, passwords, search queries or fragments.
  • Page titles are used in memory to verify the foreground window. Saving titles is off by default in desktop settings.
  • Playback is reported only when an HTML audio or video element can be observed.
  • No page inputs, page text or cloud services are used.
